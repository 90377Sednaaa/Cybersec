# Proposal: <short name>

## The idea

What do you want to build or change? Three to five sentences, plain language. Link the task(s) in
`docs/TASK_PLAN.md` it replaces, extends, or adds to.

## Why

What does it improve? Pick whatever applies: demo value, a rubric line, learning value, speed, fun.
One honest sentence each.

## Cost

Roughly how long? Which tasks or dates does it move? Does it touch a frozen contract (if so, is the change
additive)? Does it need anything from another member?

## Risk

What could break, and how do we keep `main` green while you build it? What's the fallback if it doesn't
work out (hint: the baseline task it replaces is the fallback)?

## Decision

Filled in at the weekly sync: accepted / rejected, one-line reason, and the steward's note about contract
or timeline impact.
