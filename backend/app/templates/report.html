3. Methodology and framework references

Risk is rated with the OWASP Risk Rating Methodology: every finding is judged on likelihood (threat agent and vulnerability factors) and impact (technical and business factors), each rated low / medium / high, and the pair maps onto the severity tiers used in this report (Info, Low, Medium, High, Critical). Numeric scores use a 0 to 10 scale with bands: info 0.0 to 0.9, low 1.0 to 3.9, medium 4.0 to 6.9, high 7.0 to 8.9, critical 9.0 to 10.0.

Only a human assessor confirms a finding: candidates produced by the scan pipeline or the AI interpreter stay marked unverified until an assessor accepts them (the platform enforces this server-side, with who/when attribution). References: OWASP Risk Rating Methodology, OWASP Top 10, OWASP Cheat Sheet Series. Full note: docs/risk-methodology.md.
